Story inside Record Label Simulator

The Twenty-four Hundreds

The story layer that gives Gaia a lived history before the player takes over a record label. It begins as 2399 becomes 2400 and follows people whose choices become part of the world Record Label Simulator inherits.

The Twenty-four Hundreds is being developed as part of Record Label Simulator's narrative world, not as a separate parallel game or reading product.

RelationshipPart of Record Label Simulator
SettingGaia
Opening eraThe turn of 2400
StatusIn development

Why it is there

The simulation has a past.

Record Label Simulator takes place decades after the opening of The Twenty-four Hundreds. Its characters, relationships, conflicts, and history give Gaia continuity before the player's own career begins.

That material can appear through characters, quests, achievements, archives, locations, and other game-native storytelling as those parts of the game are ready.

Not a separate public release.

More of The Twenty-four Hundreds will be shown through Record Label Simulator as its story and world material become ready for public presentation.
