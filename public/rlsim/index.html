Record Label Simulator

Run the label.Shape the charts.

Lead a record label inside a living music industry. Develop talent, guide releases, set budgets and priorities, read audiences and market signals, and build careers whose history carries forward.

The player fantasy

Build a label that leaves a mark.

You are responsible for the label-level decisions. Sign and develop Creators, support Acts, decide where resources go, shape production and release strategy, and choose when to approve, redirect, pause, or reinvest.

The people inside the world do their own work. Your job is to set direction, make commitments, advance the simulation, and respond to the results.

What you do

From talent to legacy.

01

Build your roster. Sign and develop Creators, form or support Acts, and decide where the label invests.

02

Guide the music. Set creative and production priorities, assemble Tracks and Projects, and plan releases.

03

Work the market. Allocate budgets, promote releases, respond to Trends, and make timing decisions.

04

Read the response. Follow audiences, critics, charts, finances, reputation, and the consequences of earlier choices.

05

Build long-term legacy. Careers, eras, releases, relationships, and world events accumulate as time advances.

How the simulation feels

A world you can understand.

Record Label Simulator is built so important outcomes have causes you can follow. If a release rises, stalls, finds a new audience, loses momentum, or changes an Act's career, the game is designed to give you enough information to understand what contributed to it.

You can stay at the executive level and read the signals that matter, then inspect more detail when you want to understand the chain underneath.

Gaia

The industry has a world around it.

The game takes place in Gaia, a future Earth with its own cities, nations, communities, institutions, media, culture, economy, and history. Music does not release into an empty market: audiences live in places, react through changing preferences and circumstances, and carry history with them.

The Twenty-four Hundreds contributes story, characters, and history to that same world through game-native narrative surfaces.
