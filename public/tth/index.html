Story and history inside Record Label Simulator

The Twenty-four Hundreds

A story layer inside Gaia that begins as 2399 becomes 2400, following people whose lives and choices become part of the history surrounding Record Label Simulator.

The Twenty-four Hundreds is being developed as part of the game's narrative world rather than as a separate parallel product.

RelationshipPart of Record Label Simulator
SettingGaia / future Earth

StatusIn development

Why it matters

The world has a past.

Record Label Simulator takes place decades after the opening of The Twenty-four Hundreds. Its characters, relationships, conflicts, and history help give Gaia a lived-in past instead of treating the game world as a blank simulation.

That material can surface through characters, quests, achievements, archives, locations, and other game-native storytelling as development continues.

More will be revealed through the game.

Public character, story, and world material will expand as those parts of Record Label Simulator are ready to show.
