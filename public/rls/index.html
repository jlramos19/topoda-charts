The intended game

A label sim with receipts.

Players will operate a record label inside Gaia: developing creators and acts, producing music, planning releases, reading audiences and charts, and making decisions that shape a long-running career.

The database-first design means important outcomes should be explainable. If a track moves, a project lands, or an audience changes, the systems should be able to show why instead of hiding everything behind vibes and a lucky roll.
