The real-world layer

A passion-led studio with a long view.

Tópoda Charts Studios is the independent studio and public brand behind this work. It’s founded and led by JL, who sets the creative, product, and business direction.

The goal is to build original work where simulation systems, story, and worldbuilding can actually support each other. The studio may grow into a commercial business; the business model is not settled, so we won’t cosplay certainty.

The name “Tópoda” comes from hearing “top of the” through a Spanish and Dominican ear—a small linguistic bend toward “top of the charts.”
