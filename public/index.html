The world

A music industry inside a living future Earth.

Setting GAIA Future Earth
01

Audiences live somewhere. Music enters communities with their own preferences, histories, schedules, and local context.

02

The market changes. Trends, charts, promotion, critics, timing, and prior events can change what happens next.

03

History persists. Careers, releases, world events, and your label's choices accumulate over time instead of resetting after every decision.

The Twenty-four Hundreds supplies part of the game's story and history inside Gaia rather than functioning as a separate parallel product.

The studio

Built by Tópoda Charts Studios.

Tópoda Charts Studios is the independent solo studio behind Record Label Simulator. It is founded and directed by JL, who sets the creative, product, and business direction.

The studio combines simulation design, music-industry systems, story, and worldbuilding around one game. AI-assisted creative and development workflows are used under JL's direction.
