The game

You make the calls. The world does the rest.

Record Label Simulator is being built around executive-level management rather than manual busywork. Creators do their own work inside the simulation. You decide where the label commits its money, attention, timing, and support.

Advance the world, see what happened, and respond. When a release rises, stalls, finds an audience, loses momentum, or changes a career, the game is designed to let you inspect the causes instead of hiding the result behind an unexplained score.

Current development

The first public build is smaller than the full game.

Record Label Simulator is in active development for Windows. The first planned public Experimental Alpha is a focused proof of the living-world foundation in Central Bloomville. It is not the complete label-management game.

  1. Observe

    Watch Central Bloomville run as a living simulation.

  2. Inspect

    Open people and world state to understand what is happening.

  3. Save

    Store the local world state and return to it later.

No public download is available yet. This site will point to the Windows build when it is actually ready for release.

Gaia

The music industry has somewhere to live.

Gaia is the future Earth where Record Label Simulator takes place. Its cities, nations, communities, institutions, media, culture, economy, and history give the music industry a world around it instead of an empty management screen.

The Twenty-four Hundreds is part of that same narrative history. Its characters and story material are being integrated into Record Label Simulator through game-native surfaces rather than presented as a separate parallel product.
