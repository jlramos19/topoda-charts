Your role

You are the executive, not the session musician.

Creators do the creative labor. You set priorities, budgets, timing, release strategy, promotion, approvals, and the label-level decisions that shape what happens next.

Act Eras

An Era is where strategy becomes music, imagery, releases, and history.

An Act Era is a time-bounded strategic run owned by an Act. The label sets a direction, Creators make the work, the rollout puts that work into the world, and the response becomes part of the Act's Legacy.

01

Direction

Set the Act's current priorities: creative direction, participating Creators, budgets, timing, and market focus.

02

Creation

Creators develop the material. Tracks, Projects, covers, concept photography, and other media become the visible output of the work.

03

Promotion

Schedule releases, campaign material, appearances, live activity, and the other ways the Act meets the public.

04

Legacy

Audience response, critics, charts, revenue, fatigue, competition, and catalog impact determine what the Era became.

Management loop

Results have causes.

You inspect the label, market, roster, finances, releases, and world state; make a commitment; advance the simulation; then read what changed and decide whether to continue, redirect, cancel, or reinvest.

Routine information stays aggregated and decision-relevant. The CEO needs to know what an Act sold, streamed, earned, charted, or changed over a useful period—not watch every individual audience member generate a receipt.
